==========================================
QUIZ IMPORT FILE FORMAT GUIDE
==========================================

SUPPORTED FORMATS: PDF and CSV

==========================================
CSV FORMAT (RECOMMENDED - EASIEST)
==========================================

File: quiz_questions.csv
Encoding: UTF-8

Format: question,optionA,optionB,optionC,optionD,correctAnswer

Example:
-------
question,optionA,optionB,optionC,optionD,correctAnswer
"What is the capital of France?","London","Paris","Berlin","Madrid","Paris"
"What is 2+2?","3","4","5","6","4"
"Which planet is closest to the Sun?","Venus","Mercury","Earth","Mars","Mercury"
"What is the chemical symbol for Gold?","Go","Gd","Au","Ag","Au"

Notes:
- First row must be the header
- Enclose questions and options in quotes if they contain commas
- Correct answer must EXACTLY match one of the options
- Each row = 1 question

==========================================
PDF FORMAT
==========================================

File: quiz_questions.pdf

Format Options:

OPTION 1 - Standard Numbering:
1. What is the capital of France?
A. London
B. Paris
C. Berlin
D. Madrid
Answer: B

OPTION 2 - Question prefix:
Q1. What is the capital of France?
A. London
B. Paris
C. Berlin
D. Madrid
Correct Answer: B

OPTION 3 - Parentheses:
1) What is the capital of France?
A) London
B) Paris
C) Berlin
D) Madrid
Answer: B

ANSWER FORMATS (any of these work):
- Answer: B
- Correct Answer: A
- Correct: C
- Right Answer: D
- Ans: A
- *B* (asterisks around letter)
- **C** (double asterisks)

COMPLETE PDF EXAMPLE:
---
1. What is the capital of France?
A. London
B. Paris
C. Berlin
D. Madrid
Answer: B

2. What is 2+2?
A. 3
B. 4
C. 5
D. 6
Correct Answer: B

3. Which planet is closest to the Sun?
A. Venus
B. Mercury
C. Earth
D. Mars
Answer: B

==========================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==========================================

Problem: "No questions could be extracted"
Solution: 
- Check file format matches examples above
- Ensure questions have all 4 options (A, B, C, D)
- Verify correct answer line is present
- Use CSV format if PDF issues persist

Problem: "Questions extracted but answers wrong"
Solution:
- Verify correct answer EXACTLY matches option text
- Check for extra spaces in options

Problem: "Only some questions extracted"
Solution:
- Some questions may be malformed
- Remove questions with missing options or answers
- Reformat and try again
